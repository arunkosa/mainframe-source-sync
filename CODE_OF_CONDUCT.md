# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

- be respectful and constructive
- focus discussion on technical outcomes and evidence
- assume good intent, but challenge ideas rigorously
- keep feedback specific and actionable

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment or personal attacks
- discriminatory language or conduct
- deliberate disruption of project collaboration
- publishing private or sensitive information without consent

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or block contributors whose behavior undermines a safe and productive working environment.
